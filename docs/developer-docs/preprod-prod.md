# Path to Go Live: Pre-Production to Production

---


## Steps to move from Prepod To Production

### Login to the ONDC Portal

Visit the ONDC Portal and log in using your registered credentials.

If you are a new user and do not have an account, complete the Sign Up process and register yourself on the portal.

### Portal Dashboard
After logging in, navigate to the Portal Dashboard.

For New Network Participants: If your organization is not yet registered as a Network Participant (Seller, Buyer, or Technology Service Provider (TSP)), complete the registration process by selecting the appropriate participant type and providing the required organization details.

For Existing Network Participants: If your organization is already registered, review the available participant details and verify that the information is accurate and up to date.

### Steps to Complete the ONDC Go-Live Process

From the list of registered Network Participants, click Manage against the respective participant ID to access the detailed onboarding checklist and review the steps required to complete the Go-Live process.

Follow the steps below in sequence:

#### 1. Sign the NBBL Agreement: If the agreement has not been signed yet, complete the NBBL agreement signing process.

#### 2. Subscribe to Pre-Production and Generate Authorization Keys: Subscribe to the Pre-Production environment and generate the required authorization keys. Refer to the Registry & Gateway section for detailed instructions.

#### 3. Develop the Application: Develop your application in accordance with the ONDC API Contract for the domain in which you intend to go live.

#### 4. Test the Application: Validate your application using the ONDC Workbench:
https://workbench.ondc.tech/scenario

#### 5. Generate and Submit Test Logs: Generate the required test logs using the Workbench and submit them to ONDC for verification.

#### 6. Raise a QA Review Request: To submit your application for QA verification, create an Issue through the Issue Resolution Hub. The QA team will review the submitted logs and application details.

#### 7. Complete QA Approval: Once the QA team approves the submission, Task 2.a will be marked as completed by the QA team.

#### 8. Complete Network Observability: Follow the instructions provided in the Network Observability section to complete the required network observability tasks.

#### 9. Request Live QA Walkthrough: Submit a request for the Live QA walkthrough to proceed with the final live verification.

#### 10. Subscribe to Production: Once all the required onboarding and Go-Live checklist items are marked as completed, subscribe to the Production environment.
